# Voice credits

The voice files in this folder are AI-generated synthetic speech, recorded offline with VoiceStudio (https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio) running the OmniVoice model (k2-fsa/OmniVoice, Han Zhu and coauthors). One designed voice was chosen and cloned for every clip so the voice stays the same.
